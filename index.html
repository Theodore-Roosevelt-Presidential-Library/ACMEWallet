Tickets in your pocket

Put Library tickets and membership cards in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. They show up on the lock screen at arrival and scan at the door like the QR code in the confirmation email.

Find your passes

Not live yet. This page runs in demo mode with sample passes until the lookup is switched on at www.trlibrary.com. Any number and email will work here.

How it works

  1. Enter the order number or membership ID, and the email used for it.
  2. The Library's website checks both against the ticketing system.
  3. On a match, each ticket or card gets an Add to Wallet button for the phone in hand.

No account and no app to install. The pass carries the same code as the confirmation email, so either one works at the door.

What lives where

This siteThe lookup form, the buttons, the pass preview and public images. No keys, no visitor data.
www.trlibrary.comChecks the order, signs each pass and serves it. Every key stays here.
Ticketing systemThe record of orders, tickets and memberships. Read only.

<script src="https://wallet.labs.trlibrary.com/embed.js" defer></script>

Add data-kind="membership" to open on the membership tab. The first script is optional; it draws the "continue on a phone" code for visitors on a computer.

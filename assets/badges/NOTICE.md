# Wallet badge artwork

The files in this folder are **not** covered by this repository's MIT license.
They belong to Google and Apple and are here so the Library's own pages and
emails can show the official badges next to its wallet passes.

Do not edit, recolor, redraw or re-export them. Do not copy them into other
projects; download a fresh copy from the owner and accept its terms yourself.

## Google (`google/`)

"Add to Google Wallet" buttons, English (US), downloaded unaltered from
Google's brand guidelines on 2026-11-14:
https://developers.google.com/wallet/generic/resources/brand-guidelines

| File | Use |
|------|-----|
| `enUS_add_to_google_wallet_wallet-button.svg` / `.png` | Primary button. Use this one. |
| `enUS_add_to_google_wallet_add-wallet-badge.svg` / `.png` | Condensed button, for tight spaces only. |

Google's rules in brief: at least 48 px tall, 8 px of clear space on every
side, never smaller than other buttons beside it, never recolored or
free-scaled, and it must lead to a Google Wallet save link. Google Wallet is a
trademark of Google LLC.

## Apple (`apple/`)

"Add to Apple Wallet" badge, English (US and UK), from Apple's badge download
dated 2021-10-14. The Library accepted Apple's Wallet Marketing Agreement on
the download page on 2026-11-14:
https://developer.apple.com/wallet/add-to-apple-wallet-guidelines/

| File | Use |
|------|-----|
| `add-to-apple-wallet.svg` | Web pages. Apple's `US-UK_Add_to_Apple_Wallet_RGB_101421.svg`, renamed, contents unchanged. |
| `add-to-apple-wallet.png` | Email only. The same SVG rendered at three times size (456 x 144 px), not redrawn. |

The license is limited and non-transferable, covers only the Library's own
passes, and lasts only while the Library is a member of the Apple Developer
Program. If that membership ends, remove these two files.

Apple's rules in brief: use only Apple's artwork, keep clear space of one
tenth of the badge height, keep it secondary to the page's own message, and
show it only beside a pass that works in Apple Wallet. Other languages are in
Apple's download; do not translate the badge. Apple Wallet is a trademark of
Apple Inc.
